A Message from the Team Lead

Project TEDxAfrica was founded on the belief that transformative opportunities emerge when exceptional people, ideas and cultures come together with intention.

We are building an independent curation and legacy initiative where African and international voices engage not simply as participants, but as collaborators—sharing knowledge, experiencing culture, building meaningful relationships and contributing to conversations capable of shaping the future.

Our invitation is for people who believe that legacy is built through contribution, cultural understanding and relationships that endure beyond an event

If you are ready to experience Africa with purpose and become part of a long-term community committed to ideas, excellence and meaningful global collaboration,

I warmly welcome you to Project TEDxAfrica.

Our Philosophy

Built on Three Principles

We believe enduring influence is never accidental. Every Project TEDxAfrica experience is intentionally designed around three principles that transform expertise into lasting impact.

01

Ideas Before Applause

Great ideas are remembered because they change how people think. We support the development of ideas that are clear, credible and capable of creating meaningful impact.

02

Influence Through Connection

Great opportunities are built on meaningful relationships. We create opportunities for executive engagement, strategic partnerships and authentic cross-cultural collaboration.

03

Legacy Beyond the Stage

The stage is never the destination. Our goal is to help ideas, expertise and relationships continue creating value long after the event concludes.
