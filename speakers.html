Former Chief of Defence Staff, Nigeria | Defence & Security Strategist

Selection Criteria

Who We Look For

The 100 positions are not filled on the basis of fame, following, or professional seniority alone. They are filled on the basis of idea quality, intellectual readiness, and the potential for lasting contribution.

Who we look for
  • A genuinely original idea worth sharing
  • Professional credibility or meaningful lived experience
  • Intellectual readiness and openness to development
  • Commitment to the preparation process
  • Availability for required rehearsals and programme activities
Backgrounds we welcome
  • Policy and governance
  • Technology and innovation
  • Finance and enterprise
  • Arts, culture, and creative industries
  • Science, climate, and development

Selection to speak at TEDxKubwa is not purchased and should not be represented as conditional upon payment to Project TEDxAfrica.

If Project TEDxAfrica offers separate paid travel, hospitality, visibility, cultural or professional services, those must be placed on a separate page and clearly identified as independent of TEDx speaker selection.
